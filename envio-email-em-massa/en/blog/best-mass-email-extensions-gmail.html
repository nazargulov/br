The 7 best mass email extensions and tools for Gmail in 2026

Updated November 5, 2026, 7 min read

BCC sends one generic email to everyone. Platforms like Mailchimp make you upload a list, set up a domain and pay per contact. If you just want to send 50 or 300 personalized emails from your own Gmail account, a Chrome extension takes fewer steps.

We compared 7 extensions and add-ons that do this. Free limits and prices were checked on each tool's official page on November 5, 2026.

Short answer

  • Pay once, no subscription: EmailMassa.
  • Largest free volume: MailBrother (450 per day, with its signature in the footer).
  • Advanced sales campaigns: GMass.
  • Already work in Google Sheets: Mailmeteor or YAMM.

  • Free: 1 campaign per day, up to 30 campaigns total.
  • Paid: $2.99 for 30 days or $29 for 365 days, one-time, up to 500 emails/day.

2. Mailmeteor

Best for: A steady free plan if you already work in Google Sheets.

  • Type: Gmail extension plus Sheets, Docs and Excel add-ons.
  • Free: 50 emails per day, 500 per month.

3. GMass

What applies to any tool

  • Send to people who know you. Bought lists get spam reports, and it's your Gmail account that pays for it, not the extension.
  • Test with 5 contacts first. An unfilled “Hi {{name}}” is the most common mistake.
  • Add an unsubscribe option. People must be able to opt out with one click.
  • Split big lists across days. 1,200 contacts on a @gmail.com account take 3 days.
The best extension is the one that fits your volume and how you want to pay. The real sending limit is always your Gmail account's.

FAQ

What is the best Chrome extension to send mass email from Gmail?

It depends on how you send. To pay once with no subscription: EmailMassa ($2.99 for 30 days). For the largest free volume: MailBrother (450 emails/day on @gmail.com, with its signature in the footer). For advanced sales campaigns: GMass. If you live in Google Sheets: Mailmeteor or YAMM.

Is there a free extension to send mass email in Gmail?

Yes. Mailmeteor, MergeMail and BetterMerge offer 50 emails per day for free. MailBrother allows 450 per day with a footer signature. YAMM allows 20 recipients per day. EmailMassa's free plan sends 1 campaign per day, up to 30 campaigns. GMass only has a 7-day trial.

How many emails can I send per day from Gmail with an extension?

A personal @gmail.com account can send up to 500 emails per day, with or without an extension. The limit comes from Google, and no extension raises it.

Is there a mass email extension without a monthly subscription?

Yes. EmailMassa charges a one-time payment: $2.99 for 30 days or $29 for 365 days, with no automatic renewal. The other tools on this list are subscriptions.
