Questions

Clear before you send.

Does EmailMassa send from my Gmail account?

Yes. Campaigns are sent through the Gmail API from the connected Google account. Gmail sending limits and policies still apply.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. The 30-day and 365-day options are one-time payments. There is no recurring charge or automatic renewal.

Does EmailMassa read my inbox?

No. The extension requests permission to send messages, create EmailMassa labels, and access only the Google Drive files you select.

Can I send unsolicited email?

No. You are responsible for consent, applicable anti-spam laws, Gmail policies, and maintaining an accurate suppression list.
