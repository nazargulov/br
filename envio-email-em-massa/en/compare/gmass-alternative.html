GMass alternative for Gmail: EmailMassa vs GMass

Updated November 5, 2026, 4 min read

Short answer

If you send a few campaigns a month from a regular Gmail account and don't want a $20+/month subscription, EmailMassa covers the core job (personalized emails from a sheet, open and click tracking, scheduling) for $2.99 per 30 days, paid once. If you run a sales team with long multi-step sequences, GMass is the stronger tool.

Disclosure: this page is written by the EmailMassa team. We list where GMass is the better choice too.

When to pick GMass

  • You run sales sequences with several automatic follow-up steps and want a mature, feature-rich tool.
  • You need advanced campaign features and are fine paying a monthly subscription for them.
  • You want a tool with a long track record and many Chrome Web Store ratings.

When to pick EmailMassa

  • You send a campaign now and then and don't want a monthly subscription: $2.99 buys 30 days, $29 buys a year, and nothing renews on its own.
  • You want to write in the Gmail compose window and send from your own account, with merge fields from a Google Sheet, CSV or pasted list.
  • You want open and click tracking, scheduling, templates and an unsubscribe header in the same tool.

Where EmailMassa is weaker: it's newer, with fewer Chrome Web Store ratings. The free plan is for testing (30 campaigns total), not for daily free use. Follow-ups go to the campaign recipients without a "didn't reply" filter.

FAQ

Is there a free alternative to GMass?

GMass has no permanent free plan, only a 7-day trial with 50 emails per day. EmailMassa has a free plan with 1 campaign per day, up to 30 campaigns. Mailmeteor and MergeMail have free plans with 50 emails per day.

Is EmailMassa cheaper than GMass?

Yes. EmailMassa Premium costs $2.99 for 30 days or $29 for 365 days, as a one-time payment. GMass Standard costs $20 per month billed annually ($249) or $29.95 billed monthly.

Can I send more emails per day with GMass than with EmailMassa?

Not from a regular @gmail.com account. Google limits personal Gmail accounts to 500 emails per day, whichever tool you use.

Does EmailMassa have automatic follow-ups?

Yes, Premium includes follow-up emails. A follow-up goes to the campaign recipients; there is no filter for people who replied, so remove them from the list yourself.

P.S.If you only need a few campaigns a month, try the free plan first. It sends your first campaign today from your own Gmail.
