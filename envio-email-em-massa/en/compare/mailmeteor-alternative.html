Mailmeteor alternative for Gmail: EmailMassa vs Mailmeteor

Updated November 5, 2026, 4 min read

Short answer

Mailmeteor is the better pick if 50 free emails a day cover you. If you need the full Gmail limit (500/day) for a few campaigns and don't want a monthly subscription, EmailMassa costs $2.99 for 30 days, paid once, and runs inside the Gmail compose window.

Disclosure: this page is written by the EmailMassa team. We list where Mailmeteor is the better choice too.

When to pick Mailmeteor

  • You want to send every day for free and 50 emails a day is enough.
  • You work from Google Sheets, Docs or Excel and want an add-on there.
  • You want a tool with many ratings and a long track record.

When to pick EmailMassa

  • You send a campaign now and then and don't want a monthly subscription: $2.99 buys 30 days, $29 buys a year, and nothing renews on its own.
  • You want to write in the Gmail compose window and send from your own account, with merge fields from a Google Sheet, CSV or pasted list.
  • You want open and click tracking, scheduling, templates and an unsubscribe header in the same tool.

Where EmailMassa is weaker: it's newer, with fewer Chrome Web Store ratings. The free plan is for testing (30 campaigns total), not for daily free use. Follow-ups go to the campaign recipients without a "didn't reply" filter.

FAQ

Is there a cheaper alternative to Mailmeteor?

EmailMassa Premium costs $2.99 for 30 days or $29 for 365 days, as a one-time payment with no auto-renewal. Mailmeteor Starter starts at $5.99 per user per month.

How many emails can I send for free with Mailmeteor?

Mailmeteor's free plan sends up to 50 emails per day, 500 per month, according to its pricing page (checked November 5, 2026).

Does EmailMassa work with Google Sheets?

Yes. You can import a Google Sheet, a CSV file or a pasted list, and every column becomes a merge field.

What is the daily sending limit in Gmail?

A regular @gmail.com account can send up to 500 emails per day. The limit comes from Google and applies to every mail merge tool.

P.S.If you only need a few campaigns a month, try the free plan first. It sends your first campaign today from your own Gmail.
